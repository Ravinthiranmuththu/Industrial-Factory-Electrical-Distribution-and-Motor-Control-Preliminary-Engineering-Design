# Revision C design review and release gates

**Status:** PRELIMINARY / NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION.

## Important unresolved engineering checks
- Utility-approved transformer rating, HV protection, metering and connection requirements.
- Fault current at LV busbars and remote feeder ends; transformer impedance is assumed at 6%.
- Protective device Icu/Ics, selectivity, overload settings, earth-fault disconnection and arc-flash review.
- Cable installation method, ambient correction, grouping, voltage drop, short-circuit thermal withstand and CPC sizing.
- Earthing and bonding design including soil and utility earthing arrangements.
- VFD input harmonics, EMC, STO and motor cable requirements; manufacturer model selection.
- Motor starting voltage dip and star-delta suitability for mixer load.
- Functional safety and emergency stop architecture based on machinery risk assessment.
- Architectural measured plan, lighting photometric study, emergency lighting and fire interfaces.
- Itemized supplier quotations, taxes, labor and commissioning.

## GitHub release advice
Publish as a *preliminary industrial electrical engineering case study*, never as approved construction documentation. Screenshots of the actual DXF and Excel sheets are recommended.
